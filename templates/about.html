About Me

Hello! I am Sandip Karmakar, a Ph.D. scholar in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Jadavpur University, Kolkata. My expertise lies in the Internet of Things (IoT), wireless communication, embedded systems, and machine learning, with a focus on healthcare monitoring solutions. Currently, as a Junior Research Fellow, I am developing IoT-based prototypes using Raspberry Pi, LoRa, and related technologies for remote biosignal acquisition and real-time health monitoring. My research also explores Massive MIMO and RIS-assisted NOMA IoT networks for 5G and beyond. Previously, I worked as a Lecturer at Salbani Institute of Technology, where I taught electronics courses and managed computer laboratories. I have published research papers on IoT-based healthcare systems, smart parking, and secure electronic voting machines. Proficient in Python, C, machine learning, and embedded platforms, I am passionate about developing practical, intelligent solutions that create a positive real-world impact. Fluent in Bengali, English, and Hindi, I enjoy painting, swimming, and bike riding in my free time.
